# Project checks: script editor

Pasted verbatim into every script-editor brief, after the role checklist.

1. **One list, spoken twice.** The same list of traits or items is spoken in two beats, in any chapter. Evidence: both beat ids and both quoted lists. (R3-2, R3-3)
2. **Claim with no reason beat.** A chain claim in the spine has no beat that gives its reason; it is only stated, or stated on a card. Evidence: the claim id, the beat that states it, and "no reason beat". Spine mode too. (R3-4)
3. **Stipulation against an earlier claim.** A thought-experiment stipulation seems to break a claim an earlier chapter made, and no line says why it does not. Evidence: both lines with beat ids. Add only if the human picks Q3 A. (R3-7)
4. **Evidence that does not land.** A claim the argument rests on is supported only by a demo or a joke beat, and the evidence beat is not tied to it by any line. Evidence: the claim beat, the demo beat, the evidence beat, or "none". (R3-14, R3-15)
5. **Two-way claim where the argument needs one way.** A card or line states an identity ("X is Y") where a later chapter needs only one direction. Evidence: the line and the later beat that needs the weaker form. Add only if the human picks Q4 B. (R3-18)
6. **Number and agreement.** A noun in a claim whose number does not match what it names ("particle" for many, "a physical cause" for several). Evidence: the beat id and the quoted words. Writing W2. (R1-1)

# Project checks: animation supervisor

Pasted verbatim into every animation-supervisor brief, after the role checklist.

1. **Technical label.** An on-screen label uses a technical name (a gate name, a unit, a code word) that no line in its beat or earlier beats says. Evidence: time, beat id, sheet path, the label text. (R3-10)
2. **Prop crosses the frame edge.** A prop drawn as a path is cut by the frame edge. frame-sweep sees only text and images, not paths, so look at the frames. Evidence: time, beat id, sheet path. (R1-2)
3. **Picture contradicts or begs the line.** The picture claims more than the line, or shows a result the line is still arguing for (a still brain under "others fired first"; three causes on one neuron). Evidence: time, beat id, sheet path, the line. (R1-6, R1-7)
